Facility Operations & Rules

The Sedgwick County Adult Detention Facility in Wichita operates with strict protocols to ensure public safety while facilitating family connections through technology.

Video Visitation

Physical visits have been largely replaced by high-definition video conferencing. Appointments must be scheduled 24 hours in advance via the official scheduling page.

Inmate Trust Fund

Funds can be deposited for commissary, medical copays, and communications. Verify the booking number before sending any funds to ensure accuracy.
